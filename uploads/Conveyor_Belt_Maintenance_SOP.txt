================================================================================
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CONVEYOR BELT MAINTENANCE & REPLACEMENT (SOP-CV-204)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: CONVEYOR BELT REPLACEMENT PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Perform Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) on main breaker CB-104 and verify zero energy state.
2. Loosen tensioning bolts T-1 and T-2 on tail pulley using a 24mm ratchet wrench until belt tension drops to zero.
3. Remove side safety skirts and belt scraper assemblies on both loading and discharge zones.
4. Unclip the vulcanized belt joint pin or cut mechanical lacing wire using heavy-duty wire cutters.
5. Thread new heavy-duty nitrile conveyor belt (Part # CV-BLT-1200) around drive pulley, snub pulley, and tail pulley.
6. Align belt edges using laser alignment tool (Tolerance: +/- 1.5mm across 10 meter span).
7. Re-insert lacing pin, clamp joint with alignment fixture, and apply vulcanizing sealant patch.
8. Retighten tensioning bolts T-1 and T-2 until belt deflection measures 12mm under a 10kg force at mid-span.
9. Remove LOTO locks, perform 5-minute dry test run at low speed (0.5 m/s), and monitor for tracking drift.

SECTION 2: SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- MANDATORY LOTO: Never touch or service conveyor belt components without isolating breaker CB-104.
- Beware of pinch points around drive drum and tail pulley.
- Do not wear loose clothing, long hair, or jewelry near moving conveyor rollers.
