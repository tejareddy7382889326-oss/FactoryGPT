================================================================================
INDUSTRIAL CNC MILLING MACHINE (MODEL: SPINDLE-PRO 5000) - OPERATING MANUAL
================================================================================

SECTION 1: OVERHEATING DIAGNOSTICS & TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Causes of Spindle Overheating:
- Continuous high-RPM operation exceeding 12,000 RPM without adequate coolant flow.
- Clogged closed-loop coolant lines or degraded coolant fluid.
- Contaminated spindle bearing assembly or insufficient synthetic grease lubrication.
- Spindle motor fan failure or blocked air intake filters.

1.2 Corrective Maintenance Steps for Overheating:
1. Immediately press Emergency Stop (E-STOP) and allow spindle to cool for 20 minutes.
2. Check coolant reservoir fluid level. Top up with Mobilcut 100 semi-synthetic coolant if level is below MIN line.
3. Inspect coolant pump line pressure gauge (Normal reading: 4.5 to 5.2 bar).
4. Remove spindle rear shroud filter and blow out particulate debris using compressed air (max 30 PSI).
5. Inspect spindle bearing housing for abnormal mechanical vibration. If bearing temperature exceeds 85°C after 15 minutes of idle test, replace main spindle front bearings (Part # SP-BRG-9920).

SECTION 2: DAILY SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Always wear ANSI Z87.1 approved safety glasses and cut-resistant gloves during tool changes.
- Ensure spindle enclosure guard is locked before initiating automatic tool path.
- Keep emergency stop buttons clear of tools and debris.
